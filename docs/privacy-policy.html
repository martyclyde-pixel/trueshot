Privacy Policy for TruShot

Effective date: September 20, 2026

The short version: TruShot does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data, with anyone, ever. The app has no internet permission — it is technically incapable of sending anything off your device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the TruShot camera app ("TruShot," "the app," "we," "our") for Android, developed by Martin Lobban.

2. Information we do not collect

TruShot does not collect any of the following, under any circumstance:

  • Your name, email address, or any account or identity information
  • Your location
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Your photos, videos, or any other file, in any form

TruShot requests no INTERNET permission from Android. This is not a policy choice we could quietly change later without you noticing — without that permission, the operating system itself prevents the app from making any network connection at all.

3. Permissions TruShot requests, and why

  • Camera — required to take photos and record video. This is the app's entire purpose.
  • Microphone — required only to record audio alongside video. If you deny this permission, TruShot still works fully for photos and for silent video.

TruShot does not request access to your storage, contacts, location, phone state, or any other permission beyond the two listed above.

4. Where your photos and videos are stored

Every photo and video TruShot captures is written directly to your device's own photo gallery (Android's MediaStore), inside a DCIM/TrueShot folder, exactly like the stock camera app. TruShot never uploads this data anywhere.

If you enable RAW capture, the RAW (DNG) file is saved by default to a private folder that belongs only to TruShot on your device — not visible in your gallery — unless you explicitly turn on "Also save RAW to the gallery" in the app's settings. Either way, the file never leaves your device.

5. Third-party services

TruShot does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. There are no third parties to share data with, because none is collected.

6. Children's privacy

TruShot does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

7. Deleting your data

Since nothing is stored anywhere except your own device, deleting your data is entirely in your hands: delete the photos or videos from your gallery, or uninstall the app, the same as with any other file on your phone.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted at this same page with a revised effective date. Given that TruShot's entire design is built around not collecting data, we do not anticipate that changing.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to trushot.contact@gmail.com.
